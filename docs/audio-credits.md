# Audio credits

`packages/viewer/src/assets/dice-roll.mp3` is adapted from `dice-29.wav` in **2 dice roll (29 throws)** by **RPG**, published 30 August 2013 on OpenGameArt.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/2-dice-roll-29-throws
- Original archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Dice%20sound.7z
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Adaptation: retain the first 1.42 seconds, remove low-frequency rumble, raise level by 2 dB, fade the final 60 ms, encode as mono MP3.

Movement, lava and equipment cues are synthesized by the viewer and use no external recordings. All cues follow the platform `preferences.sound` setting.
