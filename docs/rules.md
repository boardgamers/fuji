Get everyone to a 🏠 village location. **You win together when everyone has arrived. You lose together if anyone reaches zero stamina or is caught by lava.**

Each player has a skill, equipment, 25 stamina and six dice (five for some skills). Read your cards and make any starting choices.

## 1. Plan together

Look at your dice and choose a destination up to **three steps away**, or stay where you are. Movement follows connected locations, never diagonally or through lava.

Discuss your chances, but **do not share exact dice values, counts or totals**. “This route looks good” is fine.

Choose a location, then **Ready to travel**. You can change your destination until everyone is ready. Neighbors must choose different destinations, unless they have nowhere else to go. With four players, your neighbors are the players on either side of you in seating order.

## 2. Reroll in silence

Destinations are now locked. Your planned distance determines your rerolls:

| Steps | Rerolls |
| ----- | ------: |
| 0     |       2 |
| 1–2   |       1 |
| 3     |       0 |

A destination marked ↻ gives one extra reroll. Skills and injuries can change your allowance.

Select **any number of dice** to reroll together. You can choose different dice each time or finish early. Do not discuss your rolls during this phase.

## 3. Use equipment

Discussion resumes. Use equipment to improve your chances or help teammates, then confirm when finished. Each card explains its effect and when it can be used, including during planning.

Used equipment is discarded unless your skill says otherwise. Finish on an equipment location to gain a card usable next round. Loaned and set-aside dice return at the end of the round.

## 4. Move and survive

Your destination's symbols determine which dice count. Add their values, not the number of dice. For example, **yellow or 6** counts all yellow dice and all sixes, each die once.

Your total must **beat both neighbors' totals**, using **your destination's symbols for everyone**. Ties fail: you stay where you are. With two players, neutral dice provide the third total.

The Gatherer can spend power bars to add +1 per bar to a player's total.

Your lead over the highest opposing total determines stamina loss. **A small lead costs more stamina; a large lead costs less.** Check the ♥ guide on the map. You can lose stamina even when staying in place.

At **20, 15, 10 and 5 stamina**, choose a new injury:

- **Arm:** cannot use equipment.
- **Eye:** lose normal and location-bonus rerolls.
- **Amnesia:** lose your skill.
- **Leg:** lose one die after this round.

Lava spreads one step at the end of each round. Crossing an eruption marker triggers an extra eruption immediately. Watch the marked locations that will be covered next.

Keep playing after reaching the village: everyone must arrive before anyone dies.
